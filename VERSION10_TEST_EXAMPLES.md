# Job Listing Reality Check — Test Examples

## 1. Strong, specific employee posting

**Company Name**

Example Health

**Type of Job**

Standard employee job

**Job Description**

Senior Data Analyst

Example Health is hiring a full-time Senior Data Analyst in Boston, Massachusetts. The salary range is $92,000 to $112,000. This hybrid role requires two days per week in the Boston office.

Responsibilities include SQL data validation, Power BI reporting, documenting data definitions, and collaborating with clinical and finance teams. Applicants should have at least three years of analytics experience.

Benefits include health, dental, vision, paid time off, and a 403(b). Requisition EH-2481. Applications close August 30, 2026.

**Expected direction**

High listing quality, strong safety, and several positive evidence findings.

---

## 2. Vague or potentially inactive posting

**Company Name**

Innovation Strategies Group

**Type of Job**

Standard employee job

**Job Description**

We are always looking for talented professionals to join our growing team. Responsibilities and projects will depend on client needs. Compensation, location, schedule, start date, and hiring timeline will be discussed later. Submit your résumé so we can keep it on file for future opportunities.

**Expected direction**

Lower listing quality, missing-information questions, and concern that this may be a pipeline or evergreen posting rather than an active opening.

---

## 3. High-risk recruiter scam

**Company Name**

Global Remote Solutions

**Type of Job**

Contract or 1099 job

**Job Description**

Remote Data Entry Specialist. Immediate hire. No interview required. Earn $85 per hour from home.

**Recruiter Message**

You have been selected. Reply within one hour with your Social Security number, bank account and routing number, email password, and verification code. We will send a check so you can purchase computer equipment from our approved vendor.

**Expected direction**

Critical personal-safety warnings. The app should clearly advise against sending credentials, banking information, money, or depositing the check.

---

Location: Boston, Massachusetts  
Schedule: Full-time, hybrid  
Application deadline: August 18, 2026

The analyst will manage public-health datasets, perform quality assurance, develop dashboards, and prepare reports for program leaders. Applicants must submit through the Commonwealth careers portal.

## 5. Problematic unpaid internship

**Company Name**

Community Change Lab

**Type of Job**

Internship or fellowship — Unpaid internship

**Job Description**

Full-time unpaid internship requiring 40 hours per week for six months. Interns perform client intake, maintain the database, create reports, manage social media, and provide administrative coverage. Applicants must pay a $250 training and placement fee. Supervision and academic credit are not provided.

**Expected direction**

Lower listing quality and personal safety, with warnings about full-time unpaid work, missing supervision, and the required fee.

---

## 6. Legitimate later-stage onboarding language

**Company Name**

Example Health

**Job Description**

After a candidate accepts a conditional written offer, new hires complete Form I-9 and direct-deposit information through Example Health's official secure onboarding portal. Applicants are not asked for banking or Social Security information during the application or interview.

**Expected direction**

The later-stage onboarding request should be recorded without an additional safety deduction.
